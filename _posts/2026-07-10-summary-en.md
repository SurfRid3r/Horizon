## [Long March 10B Achieves China's First Stage Recovery and World's First Net Recovery](https://weibo.com/7340734455/R814of1Ki) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On July 10, 2026, the Long March 10B launch vehicle lifted off from the Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site and successfully delivered satellites into orbit. Approximately six minutes after stage separation, the first stage performed a vertical return and was recovered on a sea-based platform using a net system — marking China's first successful controlled first-stage recovery and the world's first net-based rocket recovery. This milestone demonstrates China's breakthrough in reusable rocket technology, a critical capability for dramatically reducing launch costs and enabling large-scale, low-cost access to space. The net recovery approach represents a distinct innovation path compared to SpaceX's propulsive landing and Mechazilla catching system, positioning China as a serious competitor in the commercial space race. The first stage separated from the second stage approximately six minutes after liftoff and performed a controlled vertical descent to a sea-based recovery platform. The net system recovery method uses a specialized net structure to catch the returning booster, which differs fundamentally from SpaceX's grid-fin-guided propulsive landing or tower-mounted mechanical arm capture.

## [Chinese Courts Rule Game Accounts Are Inheritable, Invalidating Platform Bans](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/chinese-courts-allow-heirs-to-inherent-accounts-of-deceased-gamers-multiple-cases-spanning-years-establish-precedent-for-digital-ownership-of-games-in-game-items-and-microtransactions) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Chinese courts have established across multiple cases spanning several years that digital assets—including game accounts, in-game items, cryptocurrency, and social media operation rights—constitute inheritable property. The rulings explicitly invalidate 'no-transfer' and 'no-inheritance' clauses in platform Terms of Service, requiring platforms to facilitate account transfers to heirs while permitting reasonable fees. These rulings establish a landmark legal precedent for digital ownership and inheritance, directly challenging the standard 'no-transfer' clauses ubiquitous in tech platforms' Terms of Service worldwide. This could reshape how digital assets are treated under inheritance law and may influence future legislation and court decisions beyond China's borders. The courts distinguished between inheritable digital property (accounts, items, cryptocurrency) and non-inheritable personal privacy data (such as chat logs), with the latter to be archived by the platform rather than transferred. Platforms are permitted to charge reasonable fees for facilitating the inheritance transfer, but cannot refuse to comply with valid inheritance requests.

**Background**: Digital inheritance has been an emerging legal issue worldwide as people accumulate significant value in virtual assets across gaming, cryptocurrency, and social media platforms. Most platforms' Terms of Service historically include clauses prohibiting account transfers, sales, or inheritance, leaving heirs with no legal recourse. The Chinese Civil Code, effective January 2021, expanded the definition of inheritable property to include digital assets, providing the legal foundation for these court rulings.

## [China Imposes Temporary Export Ban on Helium Gas](https://wms.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/wmgl/art/2026/art_2a795a0d55df4cada91c9fbd2a2cc13a.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 10, China's Ministry of Commerce and the General Administration of Customs jointly issued Announcement No. 29 of 2026, implementing a temporary export prohibition on helium gas (customs commodity code: 2804290010), effective immediately upon publication. Helium is a critical input for semiconductor manufacturing, MRI cooling systems, fiber optics, and various scientific research applications, meaning this ban could significantly disrupt global technology and healthcare supply chains. As China is both a major consumer and re-exporter of helium, the restriction may exacerbate existing supply constraints and drive up prices for downstream industries worldwide, particularly in AI hardware manufacturing. The ban specifically targets helium gas under customs code 2804290010 and is authorized under the Foreign Trade Law of the People's Republic of China. The announcement states that future adjustments will be published separately, leaving open the possibility that the restriction could be modified or lifted at a later date.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 10, 13:27

**Background**: Helium is a non-renewable noble gas primarily extracted from natural gas reserves, with the United States, Qatar, and Algeria being the world's top producers. Its unique properties—extremely low boiling point, chemical inertness, and high thermal conductivity—make it irreplaceable in semiconductor fabrication, cryogenics, leak detection, and medical imaging. China has historically relied heavily on helium imports to meet domestic demand, as its own helium production capacity has been limited, though the country has been investing in helium extraction technologies in recent years.

## [SK Hynix Raises $26.5B in Record US IPO, Surges 14% on Nasdaq Debut](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/10/south-koreas-sk-hynix-raises-26-5bn-in-record-breaking-us-ipo) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 10, SK Hynix debuted on Nasdaq via American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), pricing 177.9 million ADRs at $149 each and raising approximately $26.5 billion. The offering was oversubscribed by more than 7x, and ADRs opened at around $170, jumping approximately 14% on the first day of trading. This is the largest IPO by a foreign company in US history, surpassing Alibaba's 2014 record of $25 billion, and highlights the massive capital flow driven by AI infrastructure demands. As the world's leading manufacturer of HBM chips used in NVIDIA and AMD AI GPUs, SK Hynix's blockbuster listing signals overwhelming investor confidence in the AI hardware supply chain. Globally, this IPO ranks second only to SpaceX, and the offering was driven by robust demand for high bandwidth memory (HBM) chips amid the AI boom. The ADRs briefly rose more than 15% intraday before closing at approximately a 14% premium to the offering price.

**Background**: High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) is a 3D-stacked DRAM architecture that uses through-silicon vias (TSVs) to create wide interfaces and short signal paths, enabling massive data throughput for AI accelerators like NVIDIA GPUs. American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) are negotiable securities issued in the United States by a depositary bank that represent shares of a foreign company, allowing US investors to easily trade foreign stocks. SK Hynix is a major South Korean semiconductor manufacturer and the world's dominant producer of HBM chips critical for next-generation AI computing.
