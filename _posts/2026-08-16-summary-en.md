## [Anthropic's Preliminary Q2 Revenue Jumps 14x to Over $11.5 Billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/15/anthropic-revenue-jumps-to-over-11point5-billion-in-q2-report.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

According to documents cited by Bloomberg, Anthropic's preliminary Q2 revenue exceeded $11.5 billion, up more than 14x from $787 million a year earlier and well above the $4.73 billion posted in Q1 2026. The quarter also marked a milestone as adjusted operating profit turned positive, while the company prepares for a potentially large IPO that could launch this fall. The 14x year-over-year surge signals that frontier AI commercialization is scaling at extraordinary speed, and turning adjusted operating profit positive is a key profitability milestone that strengthens Anthropic's competitive position against rivals like OpenAI and Google. A large fall IPO would rank among the biggest AI-related public listings ever and could significantly reshape capital markets' appetite for and valuation of AI companies. The figures are preliminary and still subject to revision, so final reported numbers may differ. Also note that "adjusted operating profit" excludes certain items such as stock-based compensation and other non-cash or one-time costs, so positive adjusted operating profit does not necessarily mean the company is profitable under standard accounting rules.

**Background**: Anthropic is a US AI startup founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers and is best known for its Claude AI assistant, which is trained with a safety-first methodology called Constitutional AI and guided by the principles of being helpful, honest, and harmless. Its revenue comes mainly from subscriptions and API access to Claude for individual and enterprise customers. Adjusted operating profit measures earnings from core business operations after excluding items like stock-based compensation — a metric investors commonly use to gauge underlying business health. An IPO (initial public offering) is the process by which a private company first sells shares to public investors, and strong revenue growth plus improving profitability are typically key selling points in such listings.
