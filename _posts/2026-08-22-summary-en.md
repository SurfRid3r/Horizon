## [SemiAnalysis: Open-Source LLMs Halve Catch-Up Time Each Generation](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/are-open-models-catching-up) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SemiAnalysis published an analysis quantifying how quickly open-source LLMs close the gap with closed-source frontier models, finding that the catch-up time halves with each model generation. In the current 'agent era,' Kimi K2.6 surpassed Claude Opus 4.5 in just 4.8 months, and GLM-5.2 overtook GPT-5.2 within 6 months. If open-weight models can replicate, within months, the coding and agent capabilities that drive Anthropic's $65B+ annualized revenue, the model layer risks rapid commoditization, threatening the moats of closed-source providers. This dynamic could reshape pricing, enterprise procurement, and the competitive structure of the entire AI industry. SemiAnalysis frames LLM history in three eras — early scaling, reasoning, and agents — and finds the open/closed capability gap oscillates cyclically even as overall convergence accelerates. The report cautions that benchmark parity is not the whole story: Anthropic's productization and enterprise delivery capabilities remain a durable advantage beyond raw model scores.

**Background**: The AI industry has split into closed-source labs like OpenAI and Anthropic that keep model weights proprietary, and open-weight players like Meta, Moonshot AI (Kimi), and Zhipu AI (GLM) that release weights publicly. SemiAnalysis is an independent research firm founded around 2020 by Dylan Patel, known for data-driven analysis of semiconductors and AI infrastructure. Kimi K2.6 and GLM-5.2 are recent open-weight models with strong coding, long-horizon execution, and agent capabilities — the same workload category behind Anthropic's API revenue growth.
